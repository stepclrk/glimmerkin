Look after it. Then take it somewhere strange.

Glimmerkin is a small game about caring for a creature and leading it through worlds that are never the same twice. It changes with what you feed it.

Play as a guest Create an account

Guests keep their creature in this browser. An account keeps it on every device.

01

Care

Stroke it, feed it, let it sleep. What it eats changes its colours, and it grows through four forms.

02

Quest

Step through a portal with up to three littles. Each has one skill and a few sparks. Spend them well.

03

Collect

Bring home eggs, charms, lanterns and chime stones. The nest fills up and starts to play its own tune.

For parents

  • We keep a username and a password. No email address, no real name.
  • No adverts, no purchases, and no chat between players.
  • Anyone can make an account, and delete it from the account page. The person who runs this copy of the game can reset a password or delete an account too.
