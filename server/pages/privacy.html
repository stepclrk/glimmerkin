Glimmerkin

What we keep about you

Very little. This page lists all of it.

If you play as a guest

Nothing is kept on our server. Your creature is stored in your own browser, on your own device. Clearing the browser's site data deletes it.

If you have an account

We keep three things:

  • A username. Made up by you. We ask you not to use your real name.
  • A scrambled copy of your password. It lets us check your password without being able to read it.
  • One save. Your creature, its littles and what they own, and when they last played.

While you are signed in, your browser holds a sign-in cookie. It does nothing but keep you signed in, for up to 30 days.

What we do not keep

  • No email address, real name, date of birth, address or phone number. We never ask for them.
  • No adverts, no tracking and no analytics.
  • No payments. The game is free and has nothing to buy.
  • No chat. Players cannot see or contact each other.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare carries the connection between your browser and our server, and can see what passes through it.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering, so your browser fetches font files from Google.

A backup of the accounts and saves is made each night. It is encrypted before it is stored, and kept for 14 days.

Deleting an account

Sign in, open your account and choose “Delete my account and creature”. The account, its sign-ins and its save are removed from the server straight away, and drop out of the backups within 14 days.

For parents: if your child cannot sign in, ask whoever gave you the invite code. They run this copy of the game and can delete the account for you, or reset its password.
